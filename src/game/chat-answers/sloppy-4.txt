Part: Middlegame
Topic: Tactics
Read: practise forks, pins and skewers every day
- Name the tactic where one piece attacks two pieces at once -> **A fork** | A pin | A skewer | A discovered check
- Name the tactic where a piece cannot move without exposing a more valuable piece -> **A pin** | A fork | A skewer | A promotion
- Name the tactic where a valuable piece must move and exposes a piece behind it -> **A skewer** | A pin | A fork | A zwischenzug
Topic: Pawn structure
Read: doubled, isolated and passed pawns
- What is an isolated pawn? -> **A pawn with no friendly pawns on adjacent files** ; A pawn that has reached the last rank ; Two pawns on the same file ; A pawn protected by a bishop
- What is a passed pawn? -> **A pawn with no enemy pawns in front of it or beside its path** ; A pawn on the seventh rank ; A pawn that has captured en passant ; A pawn defended by a rook
- What is a doubled pawn? -> **Two pawns of one colour on the same file** ; Two pawns side by side ; A pawn on the fourth rank ; A pawn that cannot move

Topic: King and pawn endings

- What is the opposition?: **Kings face each other with one square between them, and the side not to move gains** | Both kings in the corner | A king next to a rook | A king attacking a pawn
- When is the king usually strongest?: **When it is active and near the centre** | When it is hidden on the back rank | When it stays on its starting square | When it is next to the queen
Topic: Basic checkmates
Read: king and queen versus king, then king and rook versus king
- Which piece gives mate most easily with the king's help? -> **The queen** | A lone bishop | A lone knight | A lone pawn
- Can a king and a lone knight force checkmate? -> **No, it is a draw** | Yes, in ten moves | Yes, in fifty moves | Only on a corner square
- What is stalemate? -> **The player to move has no legal move and is not in check** | The king is in check with no escape | Both players agree to stop | A player runs out of time
Topic: Rook endings
Read: the Lucena and Philidor positions
**What is the Lucena position?** = A winning rook ending where the pawn is on the seventh rank | A drawn rook ending | A king and pawn ending | A queen versus rook ending
**What is the Philidor position?** = A drawing method for the defender in a rook ending | A winning method for the attacker | An opening trap | A checkmate pattern
**Which side usually has the advantage if a rook is behind the passed pawn?** = The side with the passed pawn | The side with the king far away | Neither side | The side with fewer pawns
