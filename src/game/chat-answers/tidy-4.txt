Topic: Special moves

Topic: Check, checkmate and draws

What are the three ways to answer a check? = Move the king, block, or capture the attacker | Move any piece, resign, or offer a draw | Castle, promote, or capture | Block, resign, or pass
After how many moves without a capture or pawn move can a player claim a draw? = 50 | 20 | 30 | 100

What is a pin? = A piece can't move without exposing a more valuable piece | A piece is captured for free | A king is trapped | Two pawns are blocked
