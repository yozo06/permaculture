Part: First Words
Topic: Greetings and farewells
Read: Say hello and goodbye aloud to someone at three different times of day.
What does buenos días mean? = Good morning | Good night | Good afternoon | Goodbye
Which phrase means see you later? = Hasta luego | Buenas noches | Mucho gusto | Por favor
What does ¿Cómo estás? mean? = How are you? | Where are you from? | What is your name? | What time is it?
When do you say buenas noches? = In the evening or at night | Early in the morning | At lunchtime | Only when leaving
Topic: Polite phrases
Read: Practise asking for something and thanking someone, using por favor and gracias each time.
What does por favor mean? = Please | Thank you | Excuse me | You're welcome
How do you answer gracias politely? = De nada | Lo siento | Con permiso | Buen provecho
Which phrase do you use to say sorry? = Lo siento | De nada | Salud | Hasta pronto
Which phrase gets a stranger's attention politely? = Perdón | Adiós | Gracias | Igualmente

Topic: Numbers one to twenty
Read: Count aloud from one to twenty, then read out the digits of a phone number.
What is siete in English? = Seven | Six | Nine | Eleven
Which number is quince? = Fifteen | Fifty | Five | Fourteen
How do you say twelve in Spanish? = Doce | Dos | Diez | Trece
What is the Spanish word for nineteen? = Diecinueve | Dieciséis | Dieciocho | Diecisiete
Part: Getting Around
Topic: Asking for directions

What does a la derecha mean? = To the right | To the left | Straight ahead | Behind you
What does todo recto mean? = Straight ahead | Turn around | Turn left | Stop here
Which word means near? = Cerca | Lejos | Detrás | Enfrente
Topic: Taxis and buses
Read: Learn the phrases for stating a destination and asking the price before you travel.
How do you say to the airport, please? = Al aeropuerto, por favor | Del aeropuerto, por favor | En el aeropuerto, por favor | Con el aeropuerto, por favor
What does ¿Cuánto cuesta? ask about? = The price | The time | The distance | The direction
What is a parada de autobús? = A bus stop | A bus ticket | A bus driver | A bus route
How do you ask the driver to stop here? = Pare aquí, por favor | Siga aquí, por favor | Salga aquí, por favor | Vaya aquí, por favor

What does la puerta de embarque mean? = The boarding gate | The check-in desk | The passport control | The baggage claim
Which word means delayed on a departure board? = Retrasado | Cancelado | Puntual | Completo
