Part: Getting started

Topic: Greetings and introductions

Read: Learn hello, goodbye and how to say your name and how you are.

• How do you say "good morning" in Spanish? = Buenos días | Buenas noches | Hasta mañana | Buen provecho
• Which phrase means "My name is Ana"? = Me llamo Ana | Te llamas Ana | Mi nombre tienes Ana | Se llama Ana
• What does "¿Cómo estás?" ask? = How are you? | Where are you? | What is your name? | How old are you?
• Which greeting is the formal way to ask "how are you"? = ¿Cómo está usted? | ¿Cómo estás? | ¿Qué tal? | ¿Qué pasa?

Topic: Numbers and the alphabet

Read: Count to 100 and practise spelling your own name aloud.

• What is "seven" in Spanish? = siete | seis | ocho | cinco
• Which number is "quince"? = 15 | 5 | 50 | 14
• How do you say 40 in Spanish? = cuarenta | cuatro | cuatrocientos | catorce
• Which Spanish letter is always silent? = h | j | v | z

Topic: Saying where you are from

Read: Practise "Soy de..." and "Es de..." with five countries.

• How do you say "I am from Mexico"? = Soy de México | Estoy de México | Tengo de México | Voy de México
• Which verb is used to give nationality? = ser | estar | tener | hacer
• What does "¿De dónde eres?" ask? = Where are you from? | Where do you live? | Where are you going? | Who are you with?
• How do you say "She is Spanish"? = Ella es española | Ella es español | Ella está española | Ella tiene española


Part: Core grammar

Topic: Nouns and gender

Read: Sort twenty everyday nouns into masculine and feminine.

• Which article goes with "casa" (house)? = la | el | los | lo
• What gender is "el problema"? = masculine | feminine | neuter | either
• How do you make "libro" plural? = libros | libroes | libras | los libro
• Which word ending is usually feminine? = -ción | -ma | -or | -o

Topic: Present tense of regular verbs

Read: Conjugate hablar, comer and vivir in all six persons.

• What is the "yo" form of hablar? = hablo | hablas | hablan | hablé
• Which ending does "nosotros" take for -ar verbs? = -amos | -emos | -an | -áis
• How do you say "they eat"? = ellos comen | ellos comemos | ellos come | ellos comís
• Which verb is conjugated like "vivir"? = escribir | beber | cantar | comprar

Topic: Ser versus estar

Read: Write ten sentences and say which "to be" each one needs.

• Which verb describes a temporary state? = estar | ser | haber | tener
• Which sentence is correct for "I am a teacher"? = Soy profesor | Estoy profesor | Tengo profesor | Hay profesor
• Why do we say "Está cansado"? = Tiredness is a temporary state | Tiredness is a permanent trait | It names a profession | It names a nationality
• Which verb gives the location of a building? = estar | ser | ir | haber


Part: Everyday situations

Topic: Ordering food and drink

Read: Role-play a restaurant order from greeting to paying the bill.

• How do you ask for the bill? = La cuenta, por favor | La carta, por favor | La mesa, por favor | El menú, por favor
• What does "una caña" usually mean in Spain? = a small draught beer | a fishing rod | a glass of water | a coffee
• Which word means "I would like"? = Quisiera | Quedaría | Querrías | Quisieron
• Which word means "vegetarian" for a man? = vegetariano | vegano | verdura | carnívoro

Topic: Asking for directions

Read: Learn left, right, straight on and the common place names.

• What does "a la derecha" mean? = to the right | to the left | straight ahead | behind you
• How would you ask where the station is? = ¿Dónde está la estación? | ¿Dónde es la estación? | ¿Cuándo está la estación? | ¿Quién está la estación?
• What does "todo recto" mean? = straight on | turn back | next to | far away
• Which phrase means "It is nearby"? = Está cerca | Está lejos | Está detrás | Está enfrente

Topic: Telling the time

Read: Say the time aloud for every hour and half hour on a clock.

• How do you say "It is three o'clock"? = Son las tres | Es las tres | Son la tres | Está las tres
• What does "y media" add to the hour? = half past | quarter past | quarter to | o'clock
• Which phrase means "at noon"? = al mediodía | a medianoche | por la tarde | en la mañana
• Why is it "Es la una" and not "Son la una"? = One o'clock is singular | One uses the plural form | Una is always masculine | It is a formal register
