Part: Script and sounds
Topic: Vowels and matras
Read: Learn the vowels and how each one attaches to a consonant as a matra
Which vowel does the letter 'आ' stand for? = Long a, as in "father" | Short i, as in "bit" | Long u, as in "food" | Short a, as in "about"
What does the matra 'ि' add to the syllable 'कि'? = The short i sound | The long ee sound | The o sound | The ai sound
Where is the matra 'ि' written relative to its consonant? = Before it, though sounded after | After it | Below it | Above it
Which word starts with the vowel 'ऊ'? = ऊपर | इमली | आम | एक
Topic: Consonant groups
Read: Learn the five stop groups from क to म and their breathy partners
Which group of consonants begins with 'क'? = Velar: क ख ग घ ङ | Dental: त थ द ध न | Labial: प फ ब भ म | Palatal: च छ ज झ ञ
Which letter is the breathy partner of 'प'? = फ | ब | भ | म
What sets the letter 'ट' apart from 'त'? = Tongue curls back against the palate | Tongue touches the teeth | Lips close together | Air passes through the nose
Which of these is a nasal consonant? = म | प | फ | ब
Topic: Conjunct letters
Read: Practise reading joined consonants like क्ष and ज्ञ
Which conjunct is made from 'क्' and 'ष'? = क्ष | ज्ञ | त्र | श्र
What does the mark '्' (halant) do to a consonant? = Removes its inherent vowel | Adds a long vowel | Marks a nasal sound | Marks the end of a sentence
Which conjunct is pronounced roughly "gya"? = ज्ञ | क्ष | त्र | द्य
How is the conjunct 'त्र' pronounced? = tra | ksha | gya | shra
