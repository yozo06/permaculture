Coffee as a commodity

* Arabica versus Robusta: read how the two species differ in taste, cost, growing conditions and end use
* Major producing countries: study output and harvest timing for Brazil, Vietnam, Colombia, Indonesia, Ethiopia and Honduras
* Crop cycle and seasons: learn flowering, harvest and shipping months, plus Brazil’s on-year and off-year pattern
* Processing methods: compare washed, natural and honey processing and how each affects quality and price
* Grading and quality: read about screen size, defect counts, cupping scores and the main origin grades
* Consumption and demand: study the biggest importing countries and trends in roast, soluble and specialty coffee

Core vocabulary
Green coffee = Unroasted beans
Differential = Premium or discount to futures
Basis = Cash price minus futures price
Contango = Far months priced above near
Backwardation = Near months priced above far
FOB = Seller delivers on board at origin port
PTBF = Differential agreed, futures price fixed later
Certified stocks = Exchange-graded coffee in approved warehouses
The physical trade

* Supply chain players: map farmers, cooperatives, exporters, trade houses, importers and roasters and what each earns
* Physical contract terms: read the standard ESCC and GCA contracts and what each clause covers
* Incoterms and shipping: practise working out who pays freight, insurance and risk under FOB, CIF and FCA
* Differentials by origin: track how premiums and discounts move for a few key origins and grades
* Price-to-be-fixed contracts: work through examples of buyer’s call and seller’s call fixing
* Warehousing and logistics: learn about containers, bag types, moisture risk and port delays
* Trade finance basics: read about letters of credit, pre-export finance and counterparty risk

Futures and options markets

* How futures work: learn margining, daily settlement, expiry and delivery on a coffee contract
* ICE Arabica and Robusta: read both contract rulebooks and compare what coffee is deliverable
* Certified stocks and delivery: study grading, age penalties and how stock levels affect spreads
* Calendar spreads and rolls: practise calculating spreads and rolling a position between months
* Arabica-Robusta arbitrage: convert both to the same unit and track the price gap over time
* Options on coffee futures: learn calls, puts, implied volatility and simple hedging structures
* Market participants: read how commercials, funds and index traders differ in motive and behaviour

Contract facts
ICE Arabica futures ticker = KC
Arabica contract size = 37,500 lb
Arabica price quote = US cents per pound
Arabica delivery months = Mar, May, Jul, Sep, Dec
ICE Robusta futures ticker = RC
Robusta contract size = 10 tonnes
Robusta price quote = US dollars per tonne
Standard coffee bag = 60 kg
Hedging and pricing

* Short and long hedges: work through how a producer and a roaster each lock in price
* Basis risk: practise examples where futures and physical prices move by different amounts
* Pricing a physical deal: build a price from futures, differential, freight, finance and margin
* Currency exposure: study how the Brazilian real and other origin currencies drive selling
* Position and book management: learn how a trader nets physical longs and shorts against futures
* Hedge accounting and margin calls: read how cash flow strain can break a sound hedge

Fundamental analysis

* Supply and demand balance: build a simple world balance sheet from production, consumption and stocks
* Key data reports: read USDA, ICO, Conab and Cecafe releases and note their schedules
* Weather risk: study frost and drought in Brazil and how El Niño and La Niña affect origins
* Commitments of Traders report: practise reading fund and commercial positioning each week
* Stocks and export flows: track certified stocks and monthly origin export numbers
* Policy and regulation: read about EU deforestation rules, tariffs and origin export policies
* Costs of production: compare farm costs across origins to estimate price floors

Trading practice and risk

* Chart and price action: practise marking trends, support, resistance and volume on coffee charts
* Position sizing: calculate contract value, tick value and risk per trade before entering
* Leverage and margin: work out how far price can move before a margin call
* Trade plan and journal: write entry, exit and stop rules, then log every trade and review
* Paper trading: run simulated futures and spread trades for a few months before using money
* Historic price shocks: study past frosts, droughts and squeezes and how the market reacted
* Broker and market access: compare ways to trade coffee from your country, with costs and rules
